Questions

You're in Ottawa. Can you really do a Toronto salon?

Yes. We're upfront about it: Corner Site Co. is based in Ottawa and has no Toronto office. Everything a salon website needs, from your services, prices, reviews and booking link to your photos, comes to us by text or email. It usually takes a few texts.

Who takes the photos?

You do, on your phone, and we make them look good. Good light and your best work is all it takes. Until they come in, we use tasteful stock photos that are clearly placeholders.

Do I have to switch booking apps?

No. Your site gets a Book button that opens the app you already use: Fresha, Square, Booksy, Vagaro, Setmore or anything else. Your clients keep booking the same way.
