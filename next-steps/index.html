Step 3 · You · about 10 minutes

Get your web address, in your own name.

Your web address is what people type to find you, like yourbusiness.ca. You buy it yourself, so it always belongs to you, whatever happens.

  1. Pick the name. Your business name, as short as you can make it. A .ca or a .com are both fine. If it is taken, add your area or what you do, like sharpbarbersottawa.ca.
  2. Buy it. We suggest Namecheap: simple screens and a fair price. Search your name, add it to your cart, and use your own email and card. GoDaddy and most other sellers work too. Please don't buy it from Cloudflare's own store: we can't connect those the simple way.
  3. Skip the extras. At checkout you will be offered hosting, email, a site builder and more. You need none of them: your hosting is included with us.
  4. Turn on auto-renew, so it can never lapse by accident. It renews once a year, at about $20.
  5. Reply with the address you bought. We set up our side, then send you the two names for step 4.

A .ca address is only for Canadians and Canadian businesses, so the seller will ask you to confirm that. A .com asks nothing.

Already have a web address? Tell us where you bought it and whether you use email on it (like you@yourbusiness.ca). If you do, we copy your email settings first and check them twice, so your email keeps working.

Your web address is always yours, and so are your photos, logo and wording. On the pay-once plan, the site and its files are yours once paid. On the monthly plan, they become yours after 12 months, or sooner if you switch to pay-once. It is all in the one-page agreement.

What if I don't like it?

Tell us what to change. You get two rounds of changes before launch, and nothing goes live until you say “approved”. If it is not for you, say so: you owe nothing and we take the draft down.

Will my email still work?

A brand-new web address has no email yet, so there is nothing to break. If you already use email on your address, tell us before you change anything in step 4, and we copy every mail setting first.

How do I change things later?

Reply to any of our emails or text us. Monthly plan: small changes included, done within 2 business days. Pay-once plan: small fixes free for 30 days after launch, then $49 per change, only if you want one.

Will it show up on Google?

We set up every page so search engines can read it, connect your site to Google Search Console and Bing, and ask you to add your new address to your Google Business Profile, which takes 2 minutes on your phone. Nobody can promise a ranking, and we won't.
