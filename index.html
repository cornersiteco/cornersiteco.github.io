FAQ

Questions

Do I pay before I see anything?

No. We build a draft first. You only pay if you want to go ahead.

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain is registered in your name. On the pay-once plan, the site and its files are yours once it’s paid; on the monthly plan, after 12 months.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo if you have one, a few photos, and 15 minutes to tell us what makes your business different.

Which option should I pick?

Pay once ($990) if you want to own it outright with no monthly bill. Pay monthly ($129, nothing upfront) if you would rather spread the cost and have us make your changes. The monthly plan has no minimum: cancel any time. Either way, add your web address (about $20/year).

Do you work outside Ottawa?

Yes. We're based in Ottawa and work with businesses across English-speaking Canada. Everything happens by email, phone or video, and you review your draft on your phone.

Can you link the booking or ordering app I already use?

Yes. Setmore, Fresha, Booksy, Square, Uber Eats, DoorDash or anything else: we add a clear button for it. Nothing changes for how you take bookings or orders.

How fast can I be live?

Your homepage draft comes within 1 business day. Once you approve the finished site, we usually have it live within 3 business days, and always within 7, as soon as your web address is set up.

Who writes the words?

We do, from your Google listing, your reviews and a short chat. You approve everything before it goes live.

What's the extra $20 a year?

That's your web address, like yourbusiness.ca. It's yours, and it renews once a year. Don't worry about setting it up: we walk you through it in a few minutes.

When do I pay?

Only once you have approved your finished site. One-time: the full $990 then. Monthly: your first $129 at launch. Your web address (about $20/year) is separate, paid directly to the registrar. By Interac e-Transfer. If a monthly payment is 30 days late, the site may be paused; your domain is always yours.

Will it help me show up on Google?

Every page gets proper titles, descriptions, business details search engines can read, and a phone-ready layout, and we connect it to your Google Business Profile. No one can honestly promise rankings, so we don't.

Can my site be in French?

Sites are built in English. If you need a bilingual site, ask us and we'll tell you honestly what we can do.
