Terms of service

The plain-language deal: what you get, what you pay, and when.

Plain-language terms for working with Corner Site Co. Last updated November 28, 2026 (new prices: $20 a month or $490 once; monthly plan: up to 2 small changes a month, site yours after 24 months).

Free draft

We build your homepage draft for free. There's no obligation: if you don't want to go ahead, you owe nothing.

Prices and payment

  • Pay once: $490 (CAD), due when you approve your finished site, before it goes live on your domain. Hosting is included with no monthly fees. Small fixes are free for 30 days after launch; later changes are $49 each, only if you ask for one.
  • Pay monthly: $20 (CAD) a month, nothing upfront. This is our launch price: if you join while it's offered, your rate stays $20 for as long as you stay on the plan. Hosting, email at your web address (forwarded to the inbox you already use) and a simple logo if you don't have one are included. Your first payment is due at launch, then monthly. Cancel any time: the site comes down and you owe nothing more. Up to 2 small changes a month are included, each done within 2 business days; more are $49 each. You can switch to paying once at any time; just ask us for the balance.
  • Payment is by Interac e-Transfer to cornersiteco@gmail.com.
  • If a monthly payment is 30 days late, the site may be paused. Your domain is always yours.
  • Your domain (web address) is registered in your name and paid to the registrar directly, about $20 a year.

What's included

Up to 5 pages written and designed for you, built for phones, with a contact form, map and Google-ready setup, and up to 2 rounds of changes before launch. Online stores, custom booking systems and other larger features are quoted separately, in writing, before any work starts.

Timing

Your homepage draft is ready within 1 business day of receiving your details. After you approve the finished site and pay, we usually have it live within 3 business days, and always within 7, once your domain is set up. Timings pause while we're waiting on your content, answers or domain access.

Ownership

Your domain, and your own photos, logo and wording, are always yours. On the pay-once plan, the site and its files are yours once it's paid. On the monthly plan, the site and its files become yours after 24 months on the plan, and you can take them anywhere; before that, they stay with us.

Your content

You confirm that the photos, logos and information you give us are yours to use, and you approve all wording before launch.

Search rankings

We set up every site so search engines can read it properly, but no one can guarantee rankings, and we don't.

Liability

We do our best to keep your site online and working, and we fix problems promptly. Our total liability for any claim is limited to the amount you've paid us in the previous 12 months.

Google Profile Care

  • Fix-up: $225 (CAD) one time, due once the fixes you approved are done. There is no monthly plan.
  • You stay the owner of your Google Business Profile. We work as a Manager, and you can remove our access at any time.
  • We don't change your hours, services or prices, or post review replies, without your approval.
  • We never buy reviews, offer anything in exchange for them, or only ask happy customers.
  • Google controls its search and Maps results and can change its features at any time. We can't guarantee rankings, calls or reviews. Corner Site Co. is not affiliated with Google.
