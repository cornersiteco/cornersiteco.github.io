A 2-minute check

Can AI see your business?

More people now ask Google's AI or ChatGPT where to go. Those answers are built from what they can read about you. Answer eight quick questions to see how readable your business is, and the one fix for each gap.

01

Do you have a website of your own?

Not just Facebook, Instagram or a booking app. AI answers quote web pages, and a social or booking page gives them little to quote.

Fix: a simple site on your own web address, with your services, prices, hours and reviews on it.

02

Does your Google listing link to that website, and does the link work?

Many listings point to an old page, a booking app, someone else's site or nowhere at all.

Fix: point the website field on your Google Business Profile to your own site, then tap it to check.

03

Are your services and prices written as text, not only in photos?

A price list inside a photo or a PDF is hard for search engines and AI to read.

Fix: list each service and price as plain text on your site.

04

Are your hours on your site, and the same as on Google?

When two places disagree, people and machines stop trusting both.

Fix: put your hours on your site and make them match your Google listing exactly.

05

Are your name, address and phone the same everywhere?

Google, your site, Facebook and directories should all say exactly the same thing.

Fix: pick one version of each and use it everywhere, down to the suite number.

06

Does your site describe your business in code search engines read?

This is called schema: a short block that states your name, address, hours and phone in a format Google reads reliably. Not sure counts as no.

Fix: add LocalBusiness schema to your site. Google documents it; any good web builder can add it.

07

Does your Google listing have real, recent photos?

Photos of your work and your space show you're open and real.

Fix: add a few real photos of your work and your storefront, and keep adding them.

08

Do you reply to your Google reviews?

Replies show an active, real owner, and Google suggests replying to reviews.

Fix: reply to recent reviews, good and bad, briefly and personally.
